About

Here I set out plainly who wrote this report, from which sources and within which limits.

Author

Hasan İbrahim Bulduk · ITU Climate Ambassador · Climate Science and Meteorological Engineering Student

I study climate science and meteorological engineering at Istanbul Technical University. As an ITU Climate Ambassador I attended 33 sessions at COP31 Antalya and brought my notes together in this report. I am developing Uray, a start-up working on weather-aware route optimization; the report mentions it only in the recommendations, as an example.

Scope and limits

The "speaker-reported" label. I was able to verify some of the figures against an independent source; others are simply values the speaker stated on stage. Where I could not verify a figure, I wrote "according to the speaker" and did not present it as hard data. The verification summary is in the appendix at the end of the report.

Scope and limits. The report is limited to the sessions I attended; it is not an official summary of the whole of COP31. Session content rests on my notes and the session reports. The interpretations are mine and are not presented as the speakers' views.

Conflict of interest

Uray is my own start-up. The report mentions it as an example, such as "weather-aware route optimization"; the findings and figures do not rely on Uray, they come from the sessions.
