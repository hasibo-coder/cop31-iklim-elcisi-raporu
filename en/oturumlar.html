Sessions

For each of the 33 sessions I attended: title, date, venue and a one-line summary. Clicking a session title opens its session report on Drive (in Turkish).

The summaries are based on my notes; they are my interpretation, not the speakers' own words. The figure labels come from the figure cards on the theme pages.

The session reports on Drive are raw field notes I filled in on the day of each event; some figures and names (for example TADIP) appear as they were before correction. The final, corrected versions are in the report.
