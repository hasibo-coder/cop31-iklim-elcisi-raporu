COP31 Through a Climate Ambassador's Eyes

The consolidated final report of the 33 sessions I attended at COP31 Antalya.

Hasan İbrahim Bulduk, ITU Climate Ambassador, climate science and meteorological engineering student

Read the reportSee the booklet and carousel

Thirty-three sessions in seven themes. I brought what I heard in the field into one report and distilled it into eight implications and 12 recommendations for Türkiye.

This is not an official summary of COP31; it is limited to the sessions I attended.

Figures I could not verify against an independent source are marked "speaker-reported" and are not presented as hard data.

Seven themes

The key message of each theme is below. Figures, labels and my recommendations are on the theme pages.

Climate Finance and Capital

Capital exists; what is missing is the bridge that links it to investment-ready projects.

How to read the figures

The label next to each figure tells you how far you can rely on it.

Verified

I checked it against an independent source and it matched.

Corrected

The figure given in the session did not match the source; I corrected it to the source. In the tables it appears in its corrected form with its final status (Verified or Speaker-reported).

Speaker-reported

Stated by the speaker. Not independently verified; I reported it as stated, without scaling it down.

Author

Hasan İbrahim Bulduk studies climate science and meteorological engineering at Istanbul Technical University. As an ITU Climate Ambassador he attended 33 sessions at COP31 Antalya and brought his notes together in this report.
